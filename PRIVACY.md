# Groove privacy policy

_Last updated: 25 October 2026_

Groove is a desktop music player that plays your YouTube Music library. It
runs entirely on your computer. There is no Groove server, no account with
us, and no analytics or tracking.

## What Groove accesses

When you connect YouTube Music, you sign in with Google and allow Groove to
manage your YouTube account (the `youtube.force-ssl` permission). Groove uses
it only to:

- read your channel name and picture, to show which account is connected;
- read your playlists and liked music, to build your library;
- search YouTube for music when you ask it to;
- add songs to your playlists, or create a playlist, when you choose
  "Add to playlist" on a song.

Groove does not upload, delete, rate or comment on anything, and does not
change your playlists except when you add a song.

## Where your data goes

Groove talks directly from your computer to Google's servers (YouTube Data
API and the YouTube player). Nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone
else. Your Google sign-in is kept on your computer, encrypted with your
operating system's protection (Windows Data Protection), so you stay signed
in. Your settings and where you left off are also stored only on your
computer.

To check for updates, Groove contacts GitHub to see whether a newer version
has been released. That request contains no personal information.

## Removing access

Sign out in Groove (Settings → Sign out) to delete the saved
sign-in and revoke Groove's access. You can also remove Groove at any time at
<https://myaccount.google.com/permissions>. Groove's stored data is in its
app data folder (`%APPDATA%\Groove`, or `%APPDATA%\b·side` if you used it before
version 1.4.0); deleting that folder removes
all of it.

## Google API Services

Groove's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the Limited Use requirements. Groove uses YouTube API Services; see
the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
